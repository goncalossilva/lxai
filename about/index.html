About Lisbon AI

/what-to-expect

Lisbon AI started as a not‑so‑quiet group chat of hackers reminiscing about LXJS and wondering why there wasn’t a European, builder‑first AI gathering. We fixed that in 2025, and we're back for more in 2026.

  • Community-run
    Organized by people who ship open-source for a living. Volunteer based.
  • Single‑track talks, multi‑track fun
    You never miss a talk, but you choose your own dinners, workshops, and hackathon projects.
  • Open-source
    Every speaker presents the code. Every talk is live-streamed.
  • Ethical by design
    Code of conduct, diverse curation, and scholarships baked in
/location
Centro Champalimaud For the Unknown. Belém, Lisbon.

A riverfront research center campus in Belém with bright interiors, clean lines and space for technical conversations to spill outside. Easy to reach from central Lisbon by train, taxi bus or rideshare.
