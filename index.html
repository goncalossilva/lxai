/what-to-expect

AI isn't the next big thing - it is the big thing.

Lisbon AI is the annual summit for people shipping AI in the real world. No hype decks, no rubber-chicken dinners — just code, talks, and hallway hacking.

Join top labs, founding teams, and engineers deploying models in production, to chat about where we are now and what's next.

Bring your laptop.

/location
Centro Champalimaud For the Unknown. Belém, Lisbon.

A riverfront research center campus in Belém with bright interiors, clean lines and space for technical conversations to spill outside. Easy to reach from central Lisbon by train, taxi bus or rideshare.

/faq
⣾ What are the dates of the conference? +

Lisbon AI 2026 takes place September 23–24, 2026.

⣾ Where is the conference being held? +

The 2026 edition of LisbonAI will be at the Center for the Unknown at Fundação Champalimaud in Lisbon. The name couldn't be more appropriate, in the current times.

⢿ Who can attend? +

Engineers, researchers, and builders shipping AI in production. Tickets are limited to around 400 attendees.

⣽ Will talks be recorded? +

Yes, most talks are recorded and shared after the event.

⣻ Can I volunteer? +

We'll open volunteer applications closer to the event. Join us on Discord to hear about that first.

⣽ Who is organizing this? +

Lisbon AI is organized by a small team of engineers and operators from around Portugal.
